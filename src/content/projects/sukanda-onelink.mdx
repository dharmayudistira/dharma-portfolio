Sukanda OneLink helps restaurants, cafés, and other food businesses order supplies from PT Diamond Food Indonesia. I contributed to its web platform as part of the team at Zero One Group. The platform was already live and expanding when I joined the web team. My main responsibility was the recipe catalogue and recipe detail pages, alongside bug fixing across the web app.

## Building the recipe experience

I built most of the recipe catalogue and detail-page experience from near the beginning, then continued working on it as the feature expanded. The flow let customers browse recipes, open their details, and see related products.

My ownership covered that recipe experience within the wider web platform, which was built and maintained by the team.

## Extending TanStack Query usage

TanStack Query was already in the codebase, with adoption still partial. I applied it to the recipe feature as I developed those pages.

I also updated or fixed existing TanStack Query usage elsewhere while resolving bugs across the app. That gave me experience both building new features with the library and working with its existing implementations in a live product.

## Supporting an expanding product

Alongside recipe development, I handled end-to-end testing and fixed bugs across the web app. These responsibilities kept me involved in the broader product as well as the feature I mainly owned.

## Product context

The wider platform [launched in July 2023 with a catalogue of 5,000+ products](https://zero-one-group.com/case-studies/enterprise-level-e-commerce-platform-serving-fmcg-businesses-1). As of October 2026, the [Sukanda OneLink website describes 9,000 F&B businesses using the platform](https://www.sukandaonelink.com/).

## What I took from the project

Sukanda OneLink strengthened my frontend development and TanStack Query skills. Building the recipe feature and maintaining existing pages gave me practice applying the same tools to both new development and fixes in a live product.
