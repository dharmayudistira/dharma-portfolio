## Consistency is not duplication

A product should feel familiar across web and mobile, but the two surfaces do not need identical layouts or interactions. Consistency comes from shared language, priorities, and behavior. Platform conventions determine how those ideas are expressed.

On the web, flexible viewports and keyboard navigation shape the interface. Mobile products must account for touch reach, operating-system navigation, and smaller windows into the same information. Treating one platform as a resized version of the other usually weakens both.

## Preserve intent at every size

Start with the job a person is trying to complete, then choose the clearest expression for each environment. Shared tokens and content models maintain continuity. Purpose-built layouts preserve usability. Product engineering connects those layers so the experience stays coherent without ignoring the strengths of the platform.
